What is TDEE?

TDEE represents the total number of calories your body burns in a day, considering your activity level.

How to Use Your TDEE:

  • To lose weight: Eat fewer calories than your TDEE.
  • To maintain weight: Eat around your TDEE.
  • To gain weight: Eat more calories than your TDEE.

Components of TDEE Calculation

TDEE Calculation involves the following primary components:

Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)

Basal Metabolic Rate or BMR is the energy usage rate at rest, i.e., when the digestive system of an individual is in an inactive state. BMR highlights the minimum energy required to maintain only the vital organs of a person.

Activity Level

The activity level factor determines the amount of physical activity undergone by a person. It includes deliberate exercise combined with related activities performed by people as a part of their daily activities or jobs. The combination of the mentioned factors is often referred to as the thermal activity level and non-exercise related thermogenesis activities (e.g., energy spent while eating, sports-like exercise, and other non-sleeping activities).

Activity Factor to Determine the Activity Levels

The activity factor refers to the value reflecting your physical activity level. It ranges from sedentary level to very active (factor 1.2 to 1.9):

  • Sedentary (Factor 1.2): For people who perform almost zero exercise and have a desk job.
  • Lightly Active (Factor 1.375): People who engage in a few light sports or exercises, roughly one to three times a week.
  • Moderately Active (Factor 1.55): People involved in moderate sports or physical activities for about three to five times weekly.
  • Very Active (Factor 1.725): People who perform hard sports or exercise six to seven times per week.
  • Super Active (Factor 1.9): This involves a hard physical job, intensive training, or a physical job twice daily.

Thermic Effect of Food

The thermic effect of food or its specific dynamic action refers to the energy consumed by the body while processing and using the food. The thermic effect constitutes about 10% of the entire food energy intake. However, the value may vary based on the type of food items you consume. For instance, protein-rich foods have a relatively high thermic effect compared to dietary fats, as fatty foods are relatively more difficult to process.

How Does a TDEE Calculator Work

Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) Calculator performs calculations or measures calories based on the following steps:

Step 1: Estimation of BMR

TDEE calculation often starts with estimating the Basal Metabolic Rate or BMR by using the Mifflin St-Jeor Equation. For this, the online tool collects a few physical characteristics of the user, which are weight, height, age, and gender.

Step 2: Activity Level Factor

In the next step, the TDEE Calculator collects a few data points related to your physical activities to determine the activity level factor.

Step 3: Multiplication

Once the calculator determines both BMR and Activity Level Factor, it multiplies the BMR by the Activity Level Factor to display the final TDEE value.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a TDEE online calculator display?

TDEE, i.e., Total Daily Energy Expenditure online calculator, measures and displays the total calories the human body burns per day. For this, the calculator considers every movement from simple breathing and brisk walking to intensive physical exercise.

Why should one use a TDEE calculator online to know his or her TDEE value?

Knowledge of TDEE value via a TDEE online calculator is crucial because it displays the number of calories required by an individual to maintain the right weight. The value serves as a baseline for both weight gain and weight loss goals.

What parameters/formulae do TDEE calculators collect/use for TDEE calculations?

Every TDEE calculator initially uses the most popular Mifflin-St. Jeor's formula according to your age, weight, height, and gender. Simultaneously, the calculator also considers the users’ Activity Multiplier factor that reflects your daily movement level and reported exercise to estimate the total calories your body burns.

How can I use the TDEE online calculator’s results to fulfill my weight goals?

Once the TDEE result is obtained by using a TDEE Calculator online, an individual consumes calories close to the TDEE value. In contrast, the person must consume fewer calories than TDEE to achieve a deficit. If an individual wants weight gain, they should consistently have more calories than the TDEE.

Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) calculators estimate the calories burned by the human body.
