What is Body Fat Percentage?

Body fat percentage is the proportion of fat to total body weight. It is an important indicator of health and fitness.

Healthy Body Fat Ranges:

  • Essential Fat: 10-13% (women), 2-5% (men)
  • Athletes: 14-20% (women), 6-13% (men)
  • Fitness: 21-24% (women), 14-17% (men)
  • Average: 25-31% (women), 18-24% (men)
  • Obese: 32%+ (women), 25%+ (men)

Maintaining a healthy body fat percentage can help reduce the risk of health issues such as heart disease, diabetes, and obesity.

What is Body Fat and How It is Related to Obesity

Body fat refers to the adipose tissue in human beings. Such tissues play a vital role in storing lipids from the human body to create energy. Besides, the tissue secretes a few necessary hormones and provides enough insulation and cushioning to the human body.

Body fat in individuals comprises essential body fat and storage body fat. As the name suggests, essential body fat serves as a basic fat level found in almost every part of the human body. This fat is vital for maintaining life and proper reproductive functions. Essential fat percentage in humans differs between males and females i.e., 2 to 5% in men while 10-12% in women. Essential body fat is mainly present in the vital organs, bone marrow, and the central nervous system.

On the other hand, storage body fat is the excess fat which the human body accumulates and stores for energy reserves. Storage body fat contributes to obesity and it is further classified as follows:

Visceral Fat

Visceral fat is found across the organs present in the abdominal cavity. This type of excessive body fat is closely associated with a relatively high risk of cardiac diseases and metabolic disorders.

Subcutaneous Fat

Subcutaneous fat is present below the skin and it contributes to your body shape. Subcutaneous fat also serves as insulation and it is often less harmful than visceral fat.

How Does a Body Fat Percentage Calculator Work

Body fat percentage calculators estimate the fat percentage of your total body weight. Such tools adopt certain formulas and consider diverse physical measurements to calculate the approximate fat percentage. Accordingly, any online calculator follows the below steps:

1. Information Collection

To start, an online calculator collects the following information from its users:

  • Gender: Ideal fat percentage range differs between males and females.
  • Weight: Weight serves as a fundamental component of every calculation related to body composition.
  • Height: Every fitness tool determines the fundamentals of body metrics by using a person’s height in combination with his weight.
  • Additional Measurements: Depending on specific brands, any BFP calculator asks for a few additional measurements, like age, waist, and hip circumference to estimate the results accurately.

2. Application of a Mathematical Equation

The calculator applies a mathematical equation or formula to your provided inputs to derive a suitable estimated body fat percentage. The formula correlates your provided measurements with the specific patterns of body fat distribution.

Benefits to Use Body Fat Percentage Calculator

BFP calculators provide an excellent way to check the current reading related to your fat percentage and let you gain actionable and precise insights into your journey to health and fitness.

Better Alternative to BMI Measurements

A prime benefit of BFP is that it focuses on the composition of the human body as compared to its total mass. The reason for this is that BFP estimates the proportion of fat mass to lean mass accumulated in various parts of your body, like the bones, organs, and muscles. Unlike BMI measurements that display results by using weight and height, body fat percentage provides a clear picture by considering that the muscles have a relatively higher density than body fat. Furthermore, BFP identifies and mitigates several potential risks and complications related to visceral fat as early as possible.

Help You Customize Your Diet Plan and Exercise

Whether you want to achieve muscle gain or fat loss, Body Fitness Percentage Calculator will be an indispensable tool to achieve your fitness goals. BFP lets you customize your exercise schedule and diet plan to focus on only muscle gain or fat loss specifically instead of simply working for your weight loss.

Motivate You During the Body Recompositing Process

Unlike overall body weight, body fat percentage reduces consistently even when your overall weight remains constant. In this way, users regularly stay motivated and make sure that their weight loss program is working effectively to boost their physique.

Boost Athletic Performance

Regular and accurate BFP readings are often helpful for athletes, like gymnasts, boxers, and runners to improve their performance. The reason is that reduction in unnecessary body fat leads to speed, endurance, agility, and strength-to-mass ratio.
