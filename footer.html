The BMI calculator is a simple and readily available tool that provides a general idea of the individual is healthy weight according to his height. However, it has to be noted that BMI is not perfect for knowing everything about your weight status. Other measurements such as body fat percentage and waist-to-hip ratio, “body shape” (hyperlink it), and other lifestyle factors involved need to be looked into as well.
